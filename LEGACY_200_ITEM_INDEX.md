# Legacy 200-Item CTS Index

Generated from the public WordPress item pages for the original CTS item set.

Use this as a reference pool for themes or seed ideas. Do not copy items mechanically into the weekly survey cycle; rewrite candidates for current clarity, orthogonality to the weekly topic, and likely participant tension.

Source pattern: https://christianthoughtsurvey.wordpress.com/item-N/

### Item 1

Less than 100% belief (full certainty) in the Gospel will not secure redemption.

- Source: https://christianthoughtsurvey.wordpress.com/item-1/
- Tags: Belief, Gospel, Redemption
- Suggested follow-up questions:
  - Do believers have a mechanism that can legitimately provide them full certainty on propositions outside their immediate sensations? What is that mechanism, and how was the reliability of that mechanism assessed?
