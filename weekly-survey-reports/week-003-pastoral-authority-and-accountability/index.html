Executive Summary

The first Week 3 report is preliminary and based on 27 complete responses. The strongest early agreement concerns disqualifying persistent manipulative or coercive leadership, requiring meaningful pastoral accountability for finances, staff authority, and confidential information, and distinguishing biblical submission from unquestioning compliance.

Respondents also show substantial support for clear complaint procedures and outside accountability, while the widest early spread appears around whether pastors exercise too little legitimate authority, whether spiritual abuse language is sometimes over-applied, and how much moral counsel pastors should give about members' major life decisions.

Because the current response count is below the preferred threshold for subgroup claims, this preliminary report should be read at the item level only. Respondent identities, respondent-level rows, and raw free-text suggestions are not published.
