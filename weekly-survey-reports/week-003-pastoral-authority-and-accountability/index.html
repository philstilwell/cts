Week 3 Complete Available-Data Report: Pastoral Authority and Accountability

A privacy-safe complete available-data report for the third revived Christian Thought Survey weekly report, based on all 27 complete participant responses available before project closure.

Project closure

Christian Thought Survey can no longer continue.

We are deeply sorry to announce that the 2026 weekly project is ending. The email platform we used experienced a bug that caused some participants to receive multiple emails, effectively spamming them. That was never intended, and we deeply apologize for the inconvenience, frustration, and loss of trust this caused.

The response from participants made clear that continuing would not be responsible. We are disappointed that we cannot carry the project forward, and we are grateful to everyone who participated, suggested items, read the reports, or otherwise helped up to this point.

Existing public reports and archives will remain available, but no further weekly surveys, invitations, reminders, or participation/newsletter requests are planned.

Complete available-data report. These aggregate results use all 27 complete Week 3 participant responses available from the SurveyOL export before project closure.

Executive Summary

The Week 3 report now uses all 27 complete participant responses available before project closure. The strongest agreement concerns disqualifying persistent manipulative or coercive leadership, requiring meaningful pastoral accountability for finances, staff authority, and confidential information, and distinguishing biblical submission from unquestioning compliance.

Respondents also show substantial support for clear complaint procedures and outside accountability, while the widest spread appears around whether pastors exercise too little legitimate authority, whether spiritual abuse language is sometimes over-applied, and how much moral counsel pastors should give about members' major life decisions.

Because 27 complete responses is below the project's preferred threshold for public item-level summaries, this report should be read as descriptive of participating respondents only. Respondent identities, respondent-level rows, and raw free-text suggestions are not published.

Participant Ballot Results

Twenty-seven participants ranked the Week 3 ballot. The top raw ballot items were public witness over political power, shared community/service/belonging, and male-only pastoral office. The project closed before any additional survey could use those rankings, so this section is preserved only as aggregate participant preference data.

Participant-Nominated Draft Items

Two text-box suggestions were reviewed for accidental identifiers. No names, email addresses, or direct contact details were found. One suggestion was polished into a credence-slider-ready end-times item; the other was excluded as not usable as a serious survey item. The aggregate draft table is preserved to document how nominations were processed before shutdown.
