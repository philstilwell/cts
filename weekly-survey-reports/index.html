Weekly Survey Reports

The collection point for weekly Christian Thought Survey reports and the four-part weekly survey process.

This page will collect the weekly Christian Thought Survey reports once the 2026 cycle begins. Each survey will focus on one doctrinal, practical, or sociological issue of interest to Christian ministers while also carrying forward participant-generated questions.

Weekly survey structure

Each Weekly Survey will include four parts:

  1. One new CTS-administered topic: 12 related survey items provided by the CTS administration, normally answered on 0-100 credence sliders.
  2. Three participant-selected questions: 3 additional questions selected from the previous week's vote by active participants. During the launch period, these slots will appear once a prior-week vote exists.
  3. A participant-generated item ballot: a list of last week's suggested items that active participants can vote on for future participant-generated questions.
  4. A future-question text box: an open field where participants can suggest questions for a later ballot.

The cycle is cumulative: suggestions submitted in one weekly survey become the next ballot, and the top active-participant selections become future participant-generated questions.

Report format

  • Issue: the weekly CTS-administered topic and the reason it was selected.
  • Administered items: the exact wording of the 12 CTS-provided survey items.
  • Participant-selected questions: the 3 additional questions chosen by active participants' previous-week vote once the cycle is active.
  • Credence results: summary statistics for 0-100 slider responses.
  • Subgroup comparisons: denominational, role, ministry-experience, or other comparisons when sample size permits.
  • Suggested-item ballot: the ranked result from voting on last week's participant suggestions.
  • Future suggestions: a summary of submitted text-box suggestions when they can be shared responsibly.
  • Data release: a link to raw or prepared data when privacy and formatting checks are complete.

Report index

Raw data will not include direct email identifiers in public files. Participant attributes may be grouped or suppressed when needed to avoid accidental identification. Free-text question suggestions may be edited, grouped, or withheld before publication to protect privacy and keep item wording usable.
