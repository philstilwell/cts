Weekly Survey Reports

The collection point for weekly Christian Thought Survey reports and the five-part weekly survey process.

This page will collect the weekly Christian Thought Survey reports once the 2026 cycle begins. Each survey will focus on one doctrinal, practical, or sociological issue of interest to Christian ministers while also carrying forward participant nominations and participant-vote-determined items.

Weekly survey structure

Each Weekly Survey will include five parts:

  1. One CTS-administered topic: 12 related survey items from the already-generated CTS topic bank.
  2. Three participant-vote-determined questions: 3 additional live survey items chosen from the previous week's participant vote. The first week will be seeded by AI because there will not yet be a prior participant vote.
  3. A participant-nominated item ballot: a list of participant-nominated survey items from the previous week, cleaned up by AI before release, that active participants can vote on for the following week.
  4. A previous-results link: a link to the primary CTS website page containing the previous week's results.
  5. A previous-results sample: a compact visual sample, such as an infographic-style summary, showing part of the previous week's results.

The cycle is cumulative: participant nominations submitted in one weekly survey are cleaned up by AI, placed on the next ballot, and the top 3 participant-vote-determined questions become live survey items the following week.

Response rule: All survey items will be credence-based slider items. The only non-slider inputs are the participant-nominated survey item text boxes.

Report format

  • Issue: the weekly CTS-administered topic and the reason it was selected.
  • Administered items: the exact wording of the 12 CTS-provided survey items.
  • Participant-vote-determined questions: the 3 additional live survey items chosen by active participants' previous-week vote, with week one seeded by AI.
  • Credence results: summary statistics for slider responses across all live survey items.
  • Subgroup comparisons: denominational, role, ministry-experience, or other comparisons when sample size permits.
  • Nominated-item ballot: the ranked result from voting on AI-cleaned participant-nominated items.
  • Previous results: links to the primary website report and a compact visual sample of the previous week's results.
  • Future nominations: a summary of submitted text-box nominations when they can be shared responsibly.
  • Data release: a link to raw or prepared data when privacy and formatting checks are complete.

Report index

Raw data will not include direct email identifiers in public files. Participant attributes may be grouped or suppressed when needed to avoid accidental identification. Free-text item nominations may be edited, grouped, or withheld before publication to protect privacy and keep item wording usable.
