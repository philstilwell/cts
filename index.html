Returning in 2026

Christian Thought Survey 2026

Christian Thought Survey is being revived as a weekly research project for Christian ministers. Each Weekly Survey will pair one CTS-administered topic with 12 related items, 3 participant-vote-determined questions, AI-cleaned item nominations, and a link plus visual sample from the previous week's results.

What changes in 2026?

Shorter surveys, faster reports, cleaner data.

The revived project keeps the original CTS concern for precise wording and fine-grained measurement, but changes the rhythm to one focused topic, participant-guided questions, and visible feedback from the previous week's results.

  • Focused weekly surveys: each survey begins with one CTS-administered topic and 12 related items from the already-generated topic bank.
  • Participant-vote-determined questions: each survey adds 3 live items selected from active participants' previous-week vote; the first week will be seeded by AI.
  • Credence-based responses: all survey items use sliders so responses preserve more precision than ordinary agree/disagree choices.
  • Minister-focused panel: the first invitations will go to prior CTS participants who were willing to be contacted by email.
  • Open reporting: weekly summaries will be written for public reading, while data releases will be structured for responsible reanalysis.
  • Ongoing item voting: every weekly survey includes AI-cleaned participant nominations for voting and text boxes for future nominations.

Weekly structure

Every survey has a five-part rhythm.

The CTS administration supplies the main topic, active participants help choose the next participant-generated items, and each survey points back to the previous week's public results.

  1. One CTS-administered topic: 12 related survey items from the already-generated CTS topic bank.
  2. Three participant-vote-determined questions: 3 additional live survey items chosen from the previous week's participant vote. The first week will be seeded by AI because there will not yet be a prior participant vote.
  3. A participant-nominated item ballot: a list of participant-nominated survey items from the previous week, cleaned up by AI before release, that active participants can vote on for the following week.
  4. A previous-results link: a link to the primary CTS website page containing the previous week's results.
  5. A previous-results sample: a compact visual sample, such as an infographic-style summary, showing part of the previous week's results.

Response rule: All survey items will be credence-based slider items. The only non-slider inputs are the participant-nominated survey item text boxes.

Current status

The 2026 weekly cycle is being prepared.

Reports are planned for Fridays so pastors and other ministry leaders can reflect on the results before Sunday. Public summaries will be posted here, and the project is being designed so appropriately prepared raw data can be shared for independent analysis.

When the first weekly survey is fielded, this site will point to the current survey, the current report, the participant-vote-determined question vote, the AI-cleaned nominated-item ballot, a sample visual summary of the previous week's results, and the raw-data download policy.

The original 2022-2024 CTS materials remain available in the archive. They are being kept as a reference library while the front of the site shifts toward weekly reports.
