# Week 1 Independent Item Audit

Audit date: June 2, 2026

Weekly CTS-administered topic: Divorce and Remarriage

This audit documents why the Week 1 independent live items and seeded participant-nominated ballot items are suitable for the revived weekly format. Week 1 uses CTS/AI seed items because there was no previous weekly participant ballot.

## Audit Criteria

- Clarity: the item has a clear semantic target and can be rated quickly.
- Orthogonality: the item is independent from the Divorce and Remarriage topic.
- Non-duplication: the item is not copied from the current 50-topic bank or from the 12 Week 1 topic items.
- Tension potential: the item is likely to produce meaningful disagreement or response spread.
- Current relevance: the item connects to current Christian doctrine, practice, ministry, or moral judgment.
